BMW M5 CS

    The BMW M5 CS is a limited-edition, high-performance version of the M5 that blends luxury with extreme power and track-focused engineering. Introduced in 2021, it is the most powerful production car ever built by BMW’s M division, featuring a 4.4-litre twin-turbo V8 engine producing around 635 horsepower and 750 Nm of torque. This allows the car to accelerate from 0 to 100 km/h in just about 3.0 seconds, making it one of the fastest sedans in the world. What makes the M5 CS special is its focus on lightweight performance and exclusivity. BMW used carbon-fibre components and reduced weight by about 70 kg compared to the M5 Competition, improving agility and handling. The car also features an advanced all-wheel-drive system (M xDrive), sport-tuned suspension, and a luxurious interior with carbon bucket seats for four passengers. Overall, the BMW M5 CS stands out as a rare combination of everyday practicality, premium comfort, and supercar-level performance.
